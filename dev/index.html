Kraken.jl

Kraken.jl is a normal-mode model for underwater acoustic propagation, written entirely in Julia. It reimplements Michael Porter's KRAKEN from the Acoustics Toolbox, with ideas from UnderwaterAcoustics.jl.

Given a range-independent waveguide, it computes the horizontal wavenumbers and mode shapes by a finite-difference discretization of the depth-separated wave equation, then sums the modes into the acoustic pressure field. It follows KRAKEN's numerics closely: Sturm-sequence bracketing, inverse iteration, and Richardson extrapolation over successively finer meshes. It is checked against unmodified Fortran KRAKEN on every push.

What it adds is differentiability. The whole solve, from the environment through the modes to the pressure field, can be differentiated in forward or reverse mode, so group speeds, sensitivities, and gradient-based inversion come from the same code that computes the field.

Installation

using Pkg
Pkg.add("Kraken")

Quick start

using Kraken

env = UnderwaterEnv(pekeris_env()...)       # 100 m of water over a fluid half-space
sol = kraken_jl(env, 100.0)                  # modes at 100 Hz
tl = transmission_loss(sol, 1_000.0:1_000.0:5_000.0, 36.0, 55.0)   # dB, source at 36 m, receiver at 55 m
1×5 Matrix{Float64}:
 55.0176  52.4941  53.3362  55.7098  60.6207

What it models

  • Layered fluid waveguides: any number of media, each with its own sound-speed, density and attenuation profile, over a fluid half-space or a perfect boundary.
  • Boundary conditions: pressure-release, rigid, or acoustic half-space, as in KRAKEN.
  • Sound-speed interpolation: C-linear, n²-linear, or KRAKEN's not-a-knot cubic spline.
  • Attenuation in all six of KRAKEN's unit conventions, giving complex wavenumbers.
  • Fields: coherent and incoherent transmission loss, and broadband pulse synthesis.
  • Automatic differentiation: ForwardDiff for a few parameters, Zygote or Mooncake for many.

It does not model leaky modes, elastic media, or range dependence. The Validation page lists the gaps against KRAKEN alongside the measured agreement.
